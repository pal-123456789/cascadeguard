## Demo choreography (2 minutes, live link)

1. Open the live URL → header verdict reads **ALL SYSTEMS NOMINAL**.
2. Select **Connection Pool Exhaustion**, press `2` → watch Orders-DB saturation creep.
3. **Prediction halo + "PREDICTION" alert ~60–120 s before failure**; Prediction Ledger row goes green: `✓ HIT · 60s early`.
4. Failure → SEV-1 incident; Business Impact panel shows SLO burn ×, user impact %, $/min at risk.
5. Root Cause panel names **`orders-db`** (the true origin) with log evidence.
6. Recovery Optimizer recommends **`failover:orders-db`** (Monte-Carlo, 30 paired rollouts) → press **APPLY** → cascade contained.
7. Press `E` → downloads the incident report (Markdown) as a take-away artefact.
