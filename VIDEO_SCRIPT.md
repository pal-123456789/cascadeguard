## Script — CascadeGuard

**[0:00–0:12] Hook — the problem**
> "Outages don't start with a bang — they start with one saturated connection pool that quietly spreads through dependency chains until the whole system is down. CascadeGuard sees that spread *before* it happens."

**[0:12–0:34] Live demo — prediction (the money shot)**
> On the live link: pick **Connection Pool Exhaustion**, press `2`.
> "This is a live digital twin of a nine-service e-commerce cluster. Watch Orders-DB — saturation is creeping, memory is climbing. Our predictor reads only telemetry — it never sees the ground truth — and it flags Orders-DB a hundred and twenty seconds before it dies."
> (violet halo + PREDICTION alert; point at the **Prediction Ledger** row turning green: *"✓ HIT · 120s early"*).

**[0:34–0:50] Root cause + blast radius in business language**
> "When the incident opens, the root-cause analyser traces it back to the true origin — Orders-DB — not the API that screamed loudest. And it doesn't stop at service names: the Business Impact panel converts the telemetry into what a CTO asks about — SLO burn rate, percent of user flow degraded, and revenue at risk per minute."

**[0:50–1:08] Recovery — and it's quantified**
> "The recovery optimiser Monte-Carlo simulates seven strategies, thirty paired rollouts each, against a no-action baseline, and ranks them by containment probability and damage. Here it recommends failover on Orders-DB. I click Apply — and the cascade is contained. The app even exports the whole incident report as Markdown."

**[1:08–1:22] The killer metric — measured, not claimed**
> "Because the simulation knows what really breaks, CascadeGuard grades its own AI live: 100% recall — zero missed failures — 75% precision, and 100% root-cause accuracy across four fault scenarios. All of it is generated by a headless benchmark and rendered inside the product."

**[1:22–1:30] Close**
> "Built for Orcathon 2026 — a deterministic digital-twin engine, a hybrid AI stack (logistic risk scoring with trend features, graph propagation, upstream root-cause tracing, Monte-Carlo recovery), and a mission-control dashboard. Fully client-side, fully open source. Predict. Trace. Recover."

## Rules of thumb
- Screen + voice only; no intro cards, no music mixing time.
- Never show a broken state; if a feature is flaky, don't feature it.
- Speak the judge's scorecard: functionality, innovation, technical, UI/UX.
- Say the numbers out loud — they are the part judges remember.
