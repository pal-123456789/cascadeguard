Voice style: confident, clear, professional tech presenter — slightly urgent but calm (hackathon demo energy). Pace: measured but not slow.

---

[Slide 1 — Title / Hook  (0:00–0:15)]
"Outages don't start with a bang. They start with one saturated connection pool that quietly spreads through dependency chains until the whole system is down. CascadeGuard sees that spread before it happens. Built for Orcathon 2026 — Software and AI track."

[Slide 2 — The Problem  (0:15–0:30)]
"This is a live digital twin of a nine-service e-commerce cluster. The gateway feeds two APIs, two databases, a cache, a queue. When Orders DB's pool exhausts, the retry storm amplifies upstream — gateway, orders-api, inventory-api — a full cascade. CascadeGuard doesn't just alert. It predicts."

[Slide 3 — Prediction  (0:30–0:45)]
"The hybrid AI reads only telemetry — never the ground truth — and flags the origin a hundred and twenty seconds before it dies. In our flagship scenario: 120-second warning lead, 100 percent recall, 100 percent precision, zero missed failures. That's not a claim. That's the measured result from the engine's own verification loop."

[Slide 4 — Root Cause  (0:45–1:05)]
"When the incident opens, the root-cause analyser doesn't blame the loudest failure. It traces upstream: gateway to orders-api to orders-db — the true origin — using temporal precedence, self-inflicted severity, and dependency-explained share. Across all four fault scenarios: 100 percent origin accuracy. The evidence comes from structured server logs, not guesswork."

[Slide 5 — Recovery  (1:05–1:25)]
"The recovery optimizer runs Monte-Carlo: thirty paired rollouts for each of seven strategies, against a no-action baseline with common random numbers — so every lift is attributable to the action, not noise. Here: failover on orders-db achieves 100 percent containment. No-action: zero percent. Restart and circuit-breaker don't recover it. The recommendation is real, quantified, and auditable."

[Slide 6 — Business Impact  (1:25–1:40)]
"And it translates engineering telemetry into business language. At incident open: 11-point-3-7 times SLO burn rate, 100 percent of the user flow degraded, 34 thousand dollars per minute at risk. Not service names. Not abstract scores. The numbers a CTO asks for."

[Slide 7 — Measured Benchmarks  (1:40–1:50)]
"Every number on this slide is regenerated by scripts slash evaluate-dot-tee-ess — four scenarios, seed 42, 12 simulated minutes each. Average lead time: 75 seconds. Recall: 100 percent — six hits, zero missed. Origin accuracy: 100 percent. Precision: 75 percent — we report the two false alarms honestly, not smoothing them away."

[Slide 8 — Architecture + Close  (1:50–2:00)]
"A deterministic digital twin feeds a hybrid AI — logistic risk scoring with trend features, dependency-graph propagation, upstream root-cause tracing, paired Monte-Carlo recovery — rendered in a mission-control dashboard. Fully client-side. Fully open source. Predict. Trace. Recover. CascadeGuard dot ten dot vercel dot app."
